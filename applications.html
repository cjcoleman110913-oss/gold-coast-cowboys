Get Involved

Applications

However you want to be part of Gold Coast Cowboys, there's a place for you.

Volunteer

Volunteers keep the weekend running behind the scenes. Roles include registration, merch sales, evening door monitoring, staff shuttles, setup/teardown, and flooring.

Coming Soon Ambassador

Ambassadors are community builders who help spread the word in their local scene before the event, then carry that same energy onsite by creating a welcoming, social environment for dancers of every level.

Coming Soon Mentor

Mentors are experienced dancers who guide newcomers through the weekend while continuing to grow themselves. It's about connection and tone, bridging levels so the whole event feels welcoming and social.

Coming Soon Promoter

Promoters focus on visibility and reach through social content, capturing what the event actually feels like before, during, and after the weekend.

Coming Soon Vendor

Sell your products or services onsite and get direct, face-to-face time with attendees. 2026 & 2027 packages are open now.

Coming Soon Sponsor

Get your brand in front of an engaged, passionate audience in a big way. 2027 sponsorship packages are open now.
