GCC All Access Pass

Monthly Payment Terms & Conditions — 2027 Event Season

Effective Date: 10/26/2026

These Terms & Conditions govern the purchase, payment, and use of GCC All Access Passes offered by Gold Coast Cowboys (“GCC,” “Gold Coast Cowboys,” “Organizer,” “we,” or “us”) for the 2027 event season.

By purchasing a Bronze, Silver, or Gold GCC All Access Pass through an installment payment plan, the purchaser (“Customer” or “you”) agrees to these Terms & Conditions.

1. GCC All Access Pass Benefits

GCC All Access Passes provide access to designated benefits associated with eligible Gold Coast Cowboys events during the 2027 event season.

Depending on the purchased tier, benefits may include:

  • Weekend admission to designated events.
  • Jack & Jill competition entries.
  • Strictly competition entries.
  • Event merchandise, including shirts and bandanas.
  • Competition footage.
  • Workshop recap videos.
  • Intensive class admission.
  • Other benefits specifically identified in the purchased tier.

Each tier includes only the benefits expressly listed in its description at the time of purchase.

Benefits are subject to applicable event schedules, eligibility requirements, competition rules, merchandise availability, and these Terms & Conditions.

2. Down Payments & Monthly Installments

Each Bronze, Silver, and Gold GCC All Access Pass purchased through an installment plan requires an initial down payment, followed by scheduled monthly installment payments.

Before completing a purchase, the Customer will be shown the applicable total purchase price, initial down payment, installment amount, payment frequency, and scheduled payment dates.

The following payment conditions apply:

  1. The initial down payment is due at the time of purchase.
  2. The first monthly installment is due one (1) month after the initial down payment.
  3. Subsequent installments are due monthly according to the payment schedule provided at checkout.
  4. The Customer authorizes the applicable payment processing system to charge the payment method provided for the initial down payment and each scheduled installment.
  5. The Customer is responsible for maintaining a valid payment method and sufficient available funds for scheduled payments.
  6. The installment plan does not automatically renew for another event season unless the Customer separately agrees to a new purchase or payment arrangement.

The installment plan is a payment arrangement for the selected pass. It is not a month-to-month subscription that allows the Customer to retain pass benefits indefinitely while making only partial payments.

Cancellation exception: The Customer may cancel the installment plan at any time under Section 5 without being obligated to pay the remaining unpaid balance. The consequences of cancellation are described below.

3. Failed Payments & Two-Week Grace Period

If a scheduled installment payment fails, the following procedure applies:

  1. The payment processor may retry the failed payment according to its available retry settings and procedures.
  2. Gold Coast Cowboys and the Customer will be notified of the failed payment using the contact information available to the Organizer.
  3. The Customer will have fourteen (14) calendar days from the date of the initial failed payment to resolve the outstanding payment.
  4. If the payment remains unresolved after the fourteen-day grace period, all GCC All Access Pass benefits may be placed on hold.

Pass suspension applies regardless of how much the Customer has already paid. A Customer who has paid a substantial portion of the total purchase price is subject to the same suspension policy as a Customer who has made only the down payment.

While the pass is on hold, the Customer may not use associated benefits, including applicable event admission, competition entries, merchandise entitlements, workshops, competition footage, intensive classes, or other included perks that have not already been received.

A suspension is temporary and does not, by itself, cancel the payment plan.

Reinstatement of benefits: Benefits may be reinstated after the outstanding installment is successfully paid and the account is current. Reinstatement is subject to the remaining event schedule and the availability of the applicable benefits.

If the Customer does not wish to continue the payment plan, the Customer may cancel under Section 5. Upon cancellation, future installments will stop, no remaining unpaid balance will be owed, and all remaining pass benefits will be forfeited.

4. Pass Distribution & Activation

GCC All Access Passes will be distributed thirty (30) days before the applicable event.

Pass distribution and access to benefits are subject to the Customer’s account being in good standing and all required payments being current, unless otherwise approved in writing by Gold Coast Cowboys.

Customers with unresolved failed payments may have their benefits suspended under Section 3. Customers who cancel their payment plans will forfeit their remaining pass benefits under Section 5.

Receiving a physical or digital pass does not exempt the Customer from the installment payment requirements or other applicable terms.

5. Customer Cancellation of an Installment Plan

Customers may cancel their GCC All Access Pass installment plan at any time by submitting a cancellation request directly to Gold Coast Cowboys.

How to cancel:

  • Email: dance@goldcoastcowboys.com
  • Subject line: GCC All Access Pass Cancellation
  • Include the Customer’s full name and the email address used for the original purchase.

Customers should retain a copy of their cancellation request for their records. Gold Coast Cowboys will acknowledge the request and provide written confirmation once the cancellation has been processed.

Customers are not required to contact Stripe to cancel their payment plan. Gold Coast Cowboys will handle the cancellation of future scheduled charges through the applicable payment system.

Upon cancellation:

  1. Future scheduled installment payments will be stopped.
  2. The Customer will not be obligated to pay the remaining unpaid balance.
  3. All previously paid amounts, including the down payment and completed installments, will remain non-refundable, subject to applicable law.
  4. All remaining GCC All Access Pass benefits will be forfeited.
  5. The Customer will no longer be entitled to future admission, competition entries, merchandise entitlements, workshops, footage, intensive classes, or other unreceived benefits included in the pass.
  6. The Customer will not receive a refund, credit, or compensation for unused benefits, except where required by applicable law.

Cancellation does not reverse payments already collected and does not entitle the Customer to retain future pass benefits.

Gold Coast Cowboys will promptly process a valid cancellation request and stop future scheduled charges as soon as reasonably practicable. Any charges that are already in process will be reviewed and handled in accordance with applicable law and payment processing requirements.

Once cancellation has been processed, the installment plan is considered terminated. The Customer may purchase a new pass in the future, subject to availability, pricing, and the applicable terms at that time.

6. Refund Policy

Except where a refund or other remedy is required by applicable law or expressly approved in writing by Gold Coast Cowboys, payments for GCC All Access Passes are non-refundable.

This policy applies to:

  • Initial down payments.
  • Completed monthly installments.
  • Voluntary cancellation of an installment plan.
  • Pass suspension resulting from failed payments.
  • Failure to attend an event.
  • Inability to participate in an included competition, workshop, or activity.
  • Unused merchandise, footage, classes, or other benefits.
  • Personal scheduling conflicts or changes in circumstances.

Customers are responsible for reviewing event dates, locations, schedules, and included benefits before purchasing.

Failure to attend an event or use a benefit does not, by itself, entitle the Customer to a refund or credit.

Legal exception: Nothing in these Terms & Conditions waives any refund, cancellation, or other right that cannot legally be waived. Refunds or other remedies required by applicable law will be provided as required by law.

7. Non-Transferability

GCC All Access Passes are non-transferable.

Passes and their associated benefits may not be sold, assigned, gifted, or transferred to another person without prior written authorization from Gold Coast Cowboys.

Only the original purchaser or the person specifically registered to the pass may use its benefits, subject to applicable eligibility requirements and these Terms & Conditions.

Unauthorized resale, transfer, or misuse of a pass may result in suspension or revocation of the affected benefits, subject to applicable law.
