Boots on the Coast

California's Ultimate Dance Weekend

Three days. One massive dance floor. An unforgettable weekend on the coast.

Dates May 27–30, 2027
Location Irvine, CA — Venue TBD
Format 4-Day Weekend

About the Event

Boots on the Coast is Gold Coast Cowboys' flagship coastal weekend — four days built around the same western dance culture that fills country floors everywhere else, moved somewhere the sun sets over water instead of a parking lot.

Expect a full slate of workshops, socials, and competition floors, with the kind of loose, beach-town pacing that gives you room to actually enjoy the trip — not just survive it. Whether you're chasing points, chasing new material, or just chasing a good time with your dance people, this one's built for the whole spectrum.
